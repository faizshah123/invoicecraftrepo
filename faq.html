Frequently asked questions

Short answers about using InvoiceCraft Pro. For a full walkthrough see how to create an invoice.

Is InvoiceCraft Pro really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and no watermark on the PDF. The site may show Google ads after an approved AdSense account is connected so we can keep hosting the tool.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the generator, fill the form, and download. Optional saved invoices live in your browser, not in a cloud account we control.

Where is my invoice data stored?

On your device. We do not receive a copy of client names, amounts, or logos. If you need the invoice later, download the PDF or keep the browser data intact.

Can I add my logo and tax ID?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG, or SVG logo up to 2 MB. There are fields for your tax ID / VAT / NTN and the client tax ID.

Which currencies are supported?

USD, EUR, GBP, PKR, INR, AED, CAD, and AUD symbols are built into the selector. The math is the same; you choose the symbol that matches how you bill.

Does the tax field calculate VAT correctly for every country?

It applies the percentage you type to the discounted subtotal. It does not look up live tax rules. Confirm the rate and invoice layout your tax office expects.

The PDF download does nothing. What should I try?

Allow pop-ups for this site, try Chrome or Firefox, disable strict tracking blockers for the page, and use Print as a fallback. Very large logos can slow html2canvas.

Is this an official government or bank tool?

No. It is an independent website. It does not issue tax certificates or move money.

How do I contact you?

Use the contact page.
