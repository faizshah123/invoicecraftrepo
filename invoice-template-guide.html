What to include on an invoice template

A usable invoice template is a checklist, not decoration. InvoiceCraft Pro’s layouts (Modern, Classic, Minimal, Bold) change styling; the fields below are what actually get you paid.

Always include

  • The word “Invoice” and a unique invoice number.
  • Issue date and due date.
  • Seller name, address, and a way to reply.
  • Buyer name and address as they appear in the client’s books.
  • Line items with quantity, rate, and line total.
  • Currency, subtotal, tax if any, and amount due.

Include when they apply

  • Tax / VAT / GST registration numbers for seller and buyer.
  • Purchase order or contract reference.
  • Payment method and bank or wallet details.
  • Logo, if it helps recognition and stays readable in print.
  • Status such as Unpaid, Paid, or Overdue for your own copy.

Layout tips

Keep the amount due visually strongest. Put payment instructions near the total, not only in a footer people skip. Avoid light gray body text that disappears on office printers. The Minimal template is useful when a client’s AP department wants less color.

Templates vs legal forms

Some countries require specific phrases, QR codes, or sequential numbering registered with an authority. A web template cannot replace that. Use the generator for structure, then confirm local rules.
