Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to InvoiceCraft Pro (accessible at free-invoice-builders.netlify.app). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we process and how your privacy is safeguarded when using our free online invoice generator tool.

2. Client-Side Data Storage

InvoiceCraft Pro is designed as a client-side web application. All data you enter into the invoice editor — including business names, client addresses, tax identifiers, itemized costs, and uploaded logo files — is processed locally inside your browser using HTML5 LocalStorage.

We do not collect, store, or transmit your invoice data to any external server or database. Your invoice documents remain 100% private to your device.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies

We may display third-party advertisements served by Google AdSense or advertising partners to keep this service free for users.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this website. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our website or other websites on the Internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

4. Log Files & Web Analytics

Like most standard web servers, our hosting provider (Netlify) may automatically log basic non-personally identifiable network requests, such as your IP address, browser type, referring pages, and access timestamps for security and bandwidth monitoring.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email at support@free-invoice-builders.netlify.app.
