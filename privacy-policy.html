Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

InvoiceCraft Pro (“we”, “the site”) is a free invoice generator hosted at invoicecraftgen.netlify.app. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the tool.

1. Who we are

InvoiceCraft Pro is an independent web application. It is not a bank, accountant, or tax authority. For privacy questions email hello@invoicecraftgen.netlify.app.

2. Invoice data stays in your browser

The generator runs in your browser. Business names, client details, tax IDs, line items, payment notes, and logos you enter are used only to render the invoice on screen and to build a PDF on your device.

Saved drafts use HTML5 local storage on your computer or phone. We do not operate a user account system and we do not upload invoice contents to our servers.

If you clear site data, use a private window, or switch devices, those drafts will not appear. You are responsible for keeping copies of invoices you send to clients.

3. Information our host may log

The site is hosted on Netlify. Like most hosts, Netlify may record technical request data such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and time of access for security and reliability. We do not use that data to identify you for marketing.

4. Cookies and local storage

  • Necessary: local storage keys for saved invoices and a simple cookie-notice acknowledgement.
  • Advertising (if enabled): Google AdSense or similar partners may set cookies to measure ads and, where allowed, personalize them. You can opt out of personalized Google ads at Google Ads Settings or visit aboutads.info.

We do not currently run pop-up, autoplay, or third-party ad networks other than any Google-served ads you may see after an approved AdSense account is connected.

5. Google AdSense

If Google AdSense is active on this site, Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising as described above.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use invoice contents for advertising.

6. Children’s privacy

The tool is intended for adults who issue business invoices. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. International visitors

Because processing happens in your browser, invoice fields are not transferred to us. Hosting logs may be processed in the region of the content delivery network.

8. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change. The date at the top is the latest revision.

9. Contact

Email hello@invoicecraftgen.netlify.app or use the contact page.
